Blume Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Blume (Android package com.orionsoft.blume) handles, why, and what choices you have. Blume is an offline, single-player game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online multiplayer.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device only: unlocked levels, match statistics, coins, upgrades, cosmetic items, settings (sound, vibration, theme) and the optional nickname you type. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Custom scenario share codes (for example BLM-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) only contain game settings such as the map, the number of rivals and the rules. You decide whether to share them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Blume is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks) and diagnostic/crash data of the SDK.

This data is collected by Google, not by us; we do not receive data that identifies you. Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch them for an in-game bonus.

3. Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Ad personalization: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads depending on your device).
  • Some U.S. states give residents rights over how their data is used for targeted ads; Google honors these choices through the same settings.

4. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: needed to load ads.
  • Vibrate: haptic feedback in the game (can be turned off in Settings).

The game does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

5. Children

Blume is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone. The only third party that processes data in the game is Google AdMob, as described above.

7. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
